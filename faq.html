Towing & Roadside Assistance

When You Need
Help On The Road

Straight answers about requesting service, roadside assistance, pricing and what to expect when you call Southern Alberta Towing.

Are you available 24/7?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing provides 24/7 towing, recovery and roadside assistance. If you require assistance, call 403-652-0850. Please note that 24/7 service availability applies to towing, recovery and roadside assistance. Impound and vehicle release services are available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, by appointment only, excluding holidays.
What are your impound and vehicle release hours?
Impound and vehicle release services are available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, by appointment only, excluding holidays. Our 24/7 availability applies to towing, recovery and roadside assistance.
Will I receive a quote before a tow truck is dispatched?
Yes. We can provide you with a quote before dispatching a tow truck. Give us a call with your location, destination, vehicle information and a brief description of the situation, and we'll provide pricing based on the service required. Once you're ready to proceed, we can arrange the tow or service.
What information should I have ready when I call?
If possible, have your current location, vehicle type, destination and a brief description of what happened ready. For recoveries or unusual situations, additional details or photos may help us determine what equipment is required.
Do you provide roadside assistance?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing provides roadside assistance in addition to towing and recovery. Call us and let us know what is happening so we can determine the appropriate service for your situation.
I have AMA. Can I book towing or roadside assistance directly through Southern Alberta Towing?
Southern Alberta Towing is a proud AMA contractor. However, if you would like to use your AMA membership for towing or roadside assistance, your service request must be booked directly through AMA. You can request service by calling the number on the back of your AMA membership card or through the AMA mobile app. Once your request has been submitted, AMA will coordinate the service with an available contractor.
Can I call Southern Alberta Towing directly if I am not using AMA?
Yes. For private towing, roadside assistance or recovery, call us directly at 403-652-0850 to discuss the service you require and receive a quote.

Commercial & Recovery

Bigger Problems.
Serious Capability.

Commercial towing and recovery can involve very different circumstances from a standard vehicle tow. Here are some of the services Southern Alberta Towing can provide.

Do you provide accident recovery?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing provides accident recovery services for passenger vehicles and commercial vehicles. Every incident is different, so providing information about the vehicle, location and circumstances helps us determine the appropriate equipment and response.
Do you provide heavy-duty and commercial recovery?
Yes. We provide towing and recovery support for commercial vehicles and challenging roadside incidents. Call us with details about the situation so we can assess the equipment and response required.
Can you help with a vehicle that is stuck or off the road?
We provide winching and recovery services for vehicles and commercial units in a variety of situations. Because every recovery is different, information about the vehicle, location, terrain and circumstances can help us assess what will be required.
Do you work with commercial fleets and businesses?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing works with commercial operators, fleets and businesses requiring dependable towing and recovery support. Contact us to discuss your company's needs and the type of service you require.
Can you assist after a commercial vehicle accident?
Yes. Our commercial recovery capabilities include accident recovery and assistance involving commercial vehicles. The response required will depend on the circumstances of the incident, vehicle and location.
Can a business contact you about ongoing towing support?
Yes. Businesses and commercial operators can contact Southern Alberta Towing to discuss ongoing towing and recovery needs. Call 403-652-0850 or email southernalbertatowing@gmail.com .
Specialty Vehicle Transport

Not Everything
Is An Everyday Tow

Some vehicles need a different transport approach. Southern Alberta Towing provides transport options for a variety of specialty and recreational vehicles.

Do you tow classic vehicles?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing transports classic vehicles. When you call, let us know what type of vehicle you have, its condition and where it needs to go so we can determine the appropriate transport option.
Can you transport sports cars?
Yes. We provide towing and transport for sports cars and specialty vehicles. Vehicle details are helpful when booking so we can plan the appropriate equipment for the transport.
Do you tow motorcycles?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing has a dedicated motorcycle trailer for motorcycle transport. Call us with the motorcycle's location and destination to arrange service.
Do you tow holiday trailers?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing provides towing for holiday trailers. When requesting service, provide the trailer information, location, destination and details about the situation so we can assess the appropriate service.
Can you transport project or specialty vehicles?
Yes. We transport a variety of project and specialty vehicles. Because vehicle condition and transport requirements can vary, call us with the details and we'll discuss the appropriate option for your vehicle.
Can I arrange vehicle transport in advance?
Yes. Not every tow is an emergency. Contact us to discuss planned vehicle transport, your pickup location, destination and the type of vehicle being moved.

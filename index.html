View All FAQs
Will I receive a quote before a tow truck is dispatched?
Yes. We can provide a quote before dispatching a tow truck. Call with your location, destination, vehicle information and a brief description of the situation so we can provide pricing based on the service required.
I have AMA. Can I book service directly through you?
Southern Alberta Towing is a proud AMA contractor. If you would like to use your AMA membership, your service request must be booked directly through AMA using the number on the back of your membership card or the AMA mobile app.
Do you transport classic cars, sports cars and motorcycles?
Yes. We provide towing and transport for classic vehicles, sports cars and specialty vehicles. We also have a dedicated motorcycle trailer for motorcycle transport.
Do you provide accident and commercial recovery?
Yes. Southern Alberta Towing provides accident recovery and commercial recovery services. Because every recovery is different, call us with details about the vehicle, location and situation so we can assess what equipment is required.
